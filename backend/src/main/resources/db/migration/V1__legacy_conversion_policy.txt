SignalForge legacy conversion policy v1

This immutable artifact is part of migration V1's checksum.

cash:
- reject null and non-finite REAL values
- convert through BigDecimal.valueOf(double)
- book at scale 2 with HALF_EVEN

positions:
- reject null, non-finite, and negative quantity or average cost
- convert quantity through BigDecimal.valueOf(double) and strip trailing zeroes
- convert average cost to scale 2 with HALF_EVEN
- total acquisition cost is quantity multiplied by converted average cost, scale 2 HALF_EVEN

reconciliation difference sign:
- converted minus original
- audit differences retain sufficient precision and are not rounded to cash scale

identity:
- legacy listings remain unresolved, without invented venue, ISIN, or instrument type
